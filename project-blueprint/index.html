System Blueprint

Pre-Med Study Desk

A study and opportunity organiser for high school and college students heading toward medicine. Seven sections, all rendered from one data object. Open a section, read it, come back here.

The sentence that outranks everything else

“It should be organized well first.”

One component exists specifically to guarantee it: the Taxonomy Service — the Spine. It owns the single shared vocabulary and refuses to let any topic, opportunity, note or PDF be saved without valid tags. Organisation is enforced on the write path, which is the only place it can actually be guaranteed.
